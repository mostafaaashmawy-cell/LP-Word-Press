## 🚀 Quick Start Guide (Ready in 3 Minutes)

### Step 1: Install the Plugin
1. Open your WordPress Admin (`/wp-admin`).
2. Go to **Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the file `irtkaz-real-estate-crm.zip` from this folder.
4. Click **Install Now**, then click **Activate Plugin**.

### Step 2: Generate the Landing Page (1 Click)
1. In your WordPress Admin sidebar, go to **Real Estate CRM > Page Generator**.
2. Click the button: **⚡ Create Landing Page Now**.
3. Your landing page is now live at `/city-gate-new-cairo/`!
4. Click **View Live Landing Page** to see it, or click **Edit with Elementor** to customize it visually in Elementor.

## 🎨 How to Edit with Elementor (100% Free)

You can customize everything using the standard **Free Elementor** plugin:

### Option A: Edit the Generated Page
1. Go to **Pages > All Pages > City Gate New Cairo**.
2. Click **Edit with Elementor**.
3. You can click on any text, heading, or image to change prices, payment terms, or copy.
4. The lead capture form is powered by the **Real Estate Lead Form** widget located in the Elementor left panel under the **Irtkaz Real Estate** category.

### Option B: Import the JSON Template
1. Go to **Templates > Saved Templates > Import Templates**.
2. Upload `elementor-template/citygate-landing-page.json`.
3. Create a new page in WordPress, set Page Attributes > Template to **Elementor Canvas**.
4. Click **Edit with Elementor**, click the **Folder icon (Add Template)**, go to **My Templates**, and click **Insert**!

## 📊 The Leads CRM Dashboard in WordPress

When prospective buyers submit the form, where does the lead go?

Go to **Real Estate CRM > Leads & Inquiries**:
- **Live Stats Cards:** Total Leads, Today's Inquiries, Contacted, Qualified/Won.
- **Lead Contact Info:** Full Name, Phone with Country Code and Flag.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Button:** Click the green WhatsApp icon next to any lead to immediately open WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp Mobile App with a pre-filled personalized message!
- **1-Click Call Button:** Click to dial the lead directly.
- **Live Status Selector:** Switch lead status (`New`, `Contacted`, `Follow Up`, `Qualified`, `Won`, `Lost`) with instant AJAX saving.
- **Private Notes:** Click the pencil icon to record private notes on buyer budget, preferred unit, or conversation history.
- **Export to CSV:** Click **Export to CSV** in the top right to download all leads into Excel or Google Sheets.

## ⚙️ Settings & Customization

Go to **Real Estate CRM > Settings & Webhooks**:
- **Default Project Name:** Set to `City Gate New Cairo` (or change to any other project like Hyde Park, Palm Hills, etc.).
- **Agency Name:** Defaults to `Irtkaz` (or your agency name).
- **WhatsApp Sales Phone Number:** Your agent/sales phone number (e.g. `+201033373331`).
- **Email Notifications:** Toggle instant email alerts and add sales agent emails.
- **External Webhook URL:** Connect for free with Zapier, Make.com, or Google Sheets to automatically mirror leads to a CRM or spreadsheet.
